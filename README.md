# Hi Hello, I'm Kenneth!

### M.S. Bioinformatics Student | Computational Genomics & Data Science

I am an M.S. Bioinformatics student at Boston University with hands-on experience across genomics, transcriptomics, and protein structure analysis. As a Bioinformatics Technician at the UAB Dolby Lab, my work has spanned *de novo* transcriptome assembly, comparative transcriptomics, demographic modeling, venom gene characterization, spatial population inference with neural networks, candidate loci under selection, and AlphaFold-based protein structure comparison, all in HPC/SLURM environments. I'm now focused on applying machine learning and reproducible pipelines to cancer and disease research.
